DIRECT STRIKE ONLINE

Build a real base: walls, towers and generators in your construction zone, your army formation in the strip ahead of it. Every wave your whole army respawns and marches on the enemy Main Base — destroy theirs to win. Upgrade your Main Base to unlock higher unit tiers. Generators are your economy — protect them! Placement snaps to a grid (G toggles). Drag units to rearrange, right-click sells.

Camera: move the mouse to the screen edges or use arrows / WASD to scroll the map · mouse wheel zooms · Space jumps to your base · click the minimap to jump anywhere.

Starting a match goes fullscreen (press F / ⛶ to toggle). The mouse uses your real hardware cursor — no delay.
